Q7 redesign: Up Next queue front insertion bottleneck
n=1000 | ArrayList front inserts ns=825557 | ArrayDeque front inserts ns=259823
n=5000 | ArrayList front inserts ns=3187058 | ArrayDeque front inserts ns=461999
n=10000 | ArrayList front inserts ns=9003571 | ArrayDeque front inserts ns=1498459
Diagnosis: ArrayList shifts existing elements for front insertion (O(n) per insert).
Redesign: ArrayDeque supports addFirst/addLast in amortized O(1), avoiding repeated shifts.
Trade-off: ArrayDeque does not provide efficient indexed access; use it for deque/queue workloads.
